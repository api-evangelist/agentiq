# AgentIQ (Agent IQ)

> AI-powered relationship banking and institutional intelligence for banks and credit unions. The Lynq platform connects account holders with a personal banker across persistent digital channels (messaging, SMS, voice, social, web chat, video banking, co-browsing), while agentic-AI products automate staff guidance and back-office workflows. Delivered as SaaS with single-tenant deployment per institution; SOC 2 Type II and WCAG 2.1 AA. Backed by Sierra Ventures.

## Products
- [Lynq relationship banking](https://agentiq.com/lynq-relationship-banking): Core persistent relationship-banking platform
- [Lynq persistent messaging](https://agentiq.com/lynq-persistent-messaging): Always-on customer messaging
- [Lynq smart chat](https://agentiq.com/lynq-smart-chat): AI self-service chat
- [Banker Lynq](https://agentiq.com/services/banker-lynq): Persistent banker relationship contact
- [Video banking](https://agentiq.com/services/video-banking): Video banking service
- [AI for internal staff (Smart Assist / Smart Drive)](https://agentiq.com/services/ai-for-internal-staff): Generative-AI staff guidance and efficiency tools
- [AI for leadership (Smart Control)](https://agentiq.com/services/ai-for-leadership): Agentic-AI back-office workflow automation
